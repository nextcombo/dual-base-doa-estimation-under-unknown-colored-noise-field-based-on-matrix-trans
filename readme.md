# [Dual Base DOA Estimation]Under Unknown Colored Noise Field Based on Matrix Transformation for Dual-Base MIMO Radar Angle Estimation Method[With MATLAB Code]

Introduction
Multi-Input Multi-Output (MIMO) systems are a type of antenna modeling technology that utilizes multiple antennas to transmit orthogonal waveforms and average the reflected echoes. Consequently, in recent years, due to the numerous advantages of MIMO radar systems, such as increased degrees of freedom, flexibility in beam directional patterns, and improved angle diversity, they have many advantages over their direct competitors, phased array radars [1]. As a result, MIMO systems have been widely applied in target detection and positioning in the fields of radar and sonar [2].
However, these existing methods are most suitable for ideal scenarios such as orthogonal waveforms, calibrated sensor arrays, and spatially uniform white noise. However, in most MIMO radar fields, this situation may not always occur in a non-uniform noise space, and its covariance matrix is not a scaled identity matrix or a time-varying matrix. In such cases, separating the noise subspace from the signal subspace becomes an arduous task, thus violating the noise assumption and leading to performance degradation. Recently, several techniques for colored noise two-dimensionality have been proposed. In [11] and [12], spatial cross-correlation techniques are employed to eliminate spatially colored noise by leveraging the orthogonality between multiple segmented subarrays. However, with the reduction of the dimensionality of the covariance matrix, division operations inevitably result in spurious angular ambiguities. Similarly, in [13] and [14] and [18], temporal cross-correlation techniques are used to suppress spatially colored noise by assuming that the noise is uncorrelated over time, thereby exploiting the orthogonality between different temporal characteristics. Then, in [15], matrix completion techniques are applied to neutralize the non-zero noise covariances with the same index as those measured by the covariance. However, due to multi-singular value decomposition, these methods suffer from computational complexity. Moreover, assuming that the noise covariance matrix remains unchanged under a specified transformation, spatially colored noise effects can be eliminated through covariance difference [16] and [17]. However, the disadvantage is that it requires additional constraints besides resolving ambiguities.
Inspired by the statistical characteristics of the noise covariance matrix, we propose a novel denoising technique that employs an improved subspace method for angle estimation in dual-baseline MIMO radar. Initially, two distinct transformations are formulated through the enforcement of a virtual transformation on the array covariance matrix of the received signals. Subsequently, the effects of the unknown noise process are eliminated from the reformed data matrix through differential operations. This approach facilitates more convenient and predictable reassembly of the acquired measurement signals, thereby preserving certain key signal components. Subsequently, a conjugate reduced-dimension MUSIC (CRD-MUSIC) estimator is derived to estimate the joint DOD and DOA from the output of the generated virtual transformation Eigenmatrix. Therefore, fundamentally, this paper contributes by introducing a virtual transformation technique through differential programming to eliminate noise, transforming the joint estimation of two-dimensional DOD and DOA into a one-dimensional paired problem, and proposing a conjugate reduced-dimension MUSIC algorithm for high computational efficiency and performance. Finally, the derivation of the Cramer-Rao bound (CRB) for angle estimation under unknown colored noise is presented. The proposed algorithm ensures no aperture loss or signal loss, and eliminates any spurious angle estimations. Numerical simulation results validate the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed algorithm.

Conclusion
This paper considers the joint estimation of DOD and DOA in dual-baseline MIMO radar with unknown spatial color noise. A differential method based on Hermitian transformation is proposed to eliminate the effects of unknown noise and determine the angles. Firstly, two different linear transformation matrices are applied to the received measurements, resulting in two different reformed Hermitian matrices. Subsequently, the differences between the matrices are subtracted to eliminate the spatial coloring noise. Thereafter, the 2D angle estimation problem is formulated as a 1D subspace problem, where a conjugate dimensionality reduction MUSIC estimator is designed to derive the mirror joint DOD and DOA of the target. Numerical simulations validate that the proposed method can effectively eliminate color without introducing virtual aperture loss and is computationally more efficient than competing methods. However, apart from its low performance in resolving closely spaced targets analytically, the proposed method requires moderate data measurement for full effectiveness. In the future, we will focus on improving the off-line angle resolution of closely spaced targets in dual-baseline MIMO radar systems under limited measurement.
